# MellowMesh Commercial License — Outline

*For companies that want to use MellowMesh in ways the open licenses don't
permit. This is a plain-language summary to start a conversation, not the
contract itself. Contact **yannick.huchard@yaysoftware.com**.*

---

## When you need this

MellowMesh is available for free under open licenses:

- The **hub, SDKs, CLI, and protocol** — under Apache-2.0 (use it in anything,
  including commercially).
- The **relay and team control-plane** — under the **Functional Source License
  (FSL-1.1-ALv2)**: free for internal use, research, education, and professional
  services, and it converts to Apache-2.0 two years after each release.

**You need a commercial license if you want to do any of the following before
the FSL conversion date, or without the FSL's restrictions:**

1. Offer MellowMesh's relay or control-plane **as a hosted or managed service**
   to third parties ("Competing Use" under the FSL).
2. **Embed MellowMesh in a commercial product** you distribute, in a way the FSL
   or your open-source obligations don't allow.
3. Use the **"MellowMesh" name, logo, or "compatible / certified" marks** in a
   commercial offering (see `TRADEMARK.md`).
4. Obtain terms the open licenses don't provide — e.g., a **warranty, indemnity,
   or written support commitment**.

## What the commercial license grants

- A license to use the FSL-covered components for a **Competing Use** and/or to
  embed and redistribute them in your commercial product.
- An optional **trademark / OEM grant** to use the MellowMesh name or a
  "Powered by MellowMesh" / "MellowMesh Certified" mark.
- Removal of the FSL Competing-Use restriction for the licensed components,
  scoped to your product or service.
- **Warranty and liability terms** negotiated for your use (the open licenses
  are "as is").

## Structure (typical)

| Item | Approach |
|---|---|
| **Metric** | Per named deployment / product, or per developer seat — **flat annual fee**, not a per-agent or usage royalty (kept audit-free on purpose). |
| **Term** | Annual, auto-renewing; perpetual-use fallback for the version licensed if not renewed. |
| **Price anchor** *(illustrative)* | Startup / small company: **~$2–5k/yr**. Mid-market embed or hosted use: **~$10–30k/yr**. Enterprise / OEM with brand rights: **negotiated**. |
| **Support** | Standard: best-effort, community + email, no SLA (fits a lean operation). Paid SLA available as a separate add-on once We staff for it. |
| **Trademark** | Included as a named grant when brand use is part of the deal. |
| **Delivery** | Self-serve license key (issued via an automated system); no professional-services dependency. |

## What it is not

- Not a transfer of copyright — We retain ownership.
- Not a support retainer by default — support tiers are separate and optional.
- Not required for ordinary use of the Apache-2.0 hub/SDKs, or for FSL-permitted
  internal use.
