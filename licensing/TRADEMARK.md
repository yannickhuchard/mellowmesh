## You may, without asking

- **Refer to MellowMesh accurately** (nominative use): "built on MellowMesh,"

  MellowMesh protocol."
- Use the name in **blog posts, talks, tutorials, and comparisons**, provided it
  is truthful and does not imply endorsement.
- Redistribute **unmodified** official builds under their existing name.
- Use the word mark in the name of a **plugin or integration** in the form
  "X for MellowMesh" (e.g., "Acme Connector for MellowMesh"), as long as "X" is
  your own distinct brand and it is clear the plugin is not made by Us.

- Use "MellowMesh" (or a confusingly similar name) in the **name of a product,

- Use Our **logo**, or any modified version of it.
- Use marks like **"MellowMesh Certified," "Official MellowMesh," "Powered by
  MellowMesh,"** or anything implying affiliation, endorsement, or an official
  partnership.
- Name a **fork or modified distribution** "MellowMesh" or use the name in a way
  that suggests it is the official project. Forks must be **renamed** — you keep
